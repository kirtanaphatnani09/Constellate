# ✦ Constellate

### Wander freely. Connect unexpectedly.

Constellate is a personal thinking space designed to help people **wander, capture, and connect their thoughts**.

We live in an environment optimized for constant stimulation: feeds, notifications, recommendations, answers, and productivity.

Constellate explores the opposite question:

> **What happens when we create space for the mind to wander instead?**

The app does not give you ideas.

It gives you somewhere to find your own.

---

## 🌑 The Idea

Some of our most interesting thoughts appear when we aren't deliberately trying to solve anything.

While walking.

Looking out a window.

Cycling.

Listening to music.

Watching clouds.

Doing nothing.

The mind wanders, memories surface, ideas collide, and unexpected connections appear.

Most of these thoughts disappear.

**Constellate gives them somewhere to land.**

A thought becomes a **Star**.

Stars can be moved, named, connected, grouped, and rearranged.

When relationships emerge between them, they become **Constellations**.

> **A star is a thought.
> A constellation is a relationship between thoughts.**

---

# 🌌 Product Philosophy

Constellate is built around a few principles.

### 1. Don't give people stimuli. Give them space.

Constellate is not a content feed.

It doesn't exist to constantly give users something to consume.

The user brings the thought.

The app creates the conditions for it to emerge.

### 2. Wandering isn't wasted time.

Not every moment needs to be optimized.

The app intentionally creates opportunities for:

* walking
* observing
* listening
* daydreaming
* remembering
* imagining
* noticing nature
* looking up
* doing nothing

### 3. The app should disappear.

Once a wandering session begins, the phone should become secondary.

The ideal loop is:

```text
Choose
   ↓
Wander
   ↓
Put the phone away
   ↓
Notice
   ↓
Return
   ↓
Capture
```

### 4. Questions don't need answers.

An unfinished thought is still valuable.

A Star can remain unresolved indefinitely.

### 5. Connections belong to the user.

Constellate should never assume why two thoughts are related.

The user decides what belongs together.

### 6. Rearranging is thinking.

Moving thoughts around is not merely visual organization.

Changing the spatial relationship between ideas can change how we perceive them.

---

# 🧠 Cognitive Inspiration

Constellate is inspired by research into:

* mind wandering
* internally directed cognition
* the Default Mode Network (DMN)
* executive control
* creative cognition
* associative thinking

The product does **not** assume a simplistic model where:

```text
DMN = creativity
TPN = productivity
```

Instead, the conceptual model is that human cognition involves movement and interaction between different modes.

A simplified product metaphor is:

```text
WANDER
  ↓
Possibilities emerge
  ↓
CONNECT
  ↓
Relationships become visible
  ↓
FOCUS
  ↓
Something can be made
```

The neuroscience informs the design philosophy rather than becoming a gimmick inside the product.

# ✦ Core Concepts

## Wander

A period of intentionally unstructured exploration.

Users choose how they want to wander.

Examples:

* Walk
* Observe
* Listen
* Daydream
* Look up
* Look closer
* Remember
* Imagine
* Ask why
* Ask what if
* Notice nature
* People-watch
* Doodle
* Explore somewhere new
* Do nothing

Users can also create their own wandering activities.

---

## Star

A Star is something worth keeping from a wandering session.

It might be:

* a question
* an observation
* an idea
* a realization
* a memory
* a connection
* a feeling
* an unfinished thought

Example:

### ✦ The Quiet After Rain

> Why does the city feel different after it rains?

Stars can be named and renamed by the user.

---

## Connection

A relationship between two Stars.

Users can connect Stars with lines or directional arrows.

For example:

```text
Why do birds migrate?
          ↓
How does the brain know where it is?
          ↓
How do AI systems navigate?
```

The user does not need to explain the connection.

The relationship can simply mean:

> **These belong together in my mind.**

---

## Constellation

A group of Stars that the user recognizes as belonging together.

A Constellation can represent:

* a theme
* a question
* an exploration
* a project
* a period of life
* an intellectual thread
* a collection of seemingly unrelated thoughts

For example:

### 🌌 ALIVENESS

```text
What makes something alive?
          ↘
Why do trees behave collectively?
          ↓
Can AI be alive?
          ↗
Why does nature feel intelligent?
```

A Star can belong to more than one Constellation.

This means Constellate is not a folder system.

It is a **relationship system**.

# 🧭 Wander Compass

The primary interaction is a **Wander Compass** rather than a traditional spinning wheel.

The user sees different ways of wandering and chooses one.

```text
                  LOOK UP

       OBSERVE              DAYDREAM


  WALK             ✦              LISTEN


       REMEMBER             IMAGINE

                 DO NOTHING
```

The user has agency over the experience.

An optional **Surprise Me** function can randomly choose an activity for people who don't want to decide.

---

# 🌌 Your Sky

Your Sky is the user's personal thinking canvas.

It is intentionally different from a conventional notes list.

Users can:

* create Stars
* name Stars
* move Stars
* connect Stars
* draw directional arrows
* group Stars
* encircle Stars
* create Constellations
* move Constellations
* rename Constellations
* expand and collapse Constellations
* rearrange their entire Sky

The Sky is not meant to be objectively organized.

It represents **the user's own way of seeing relationships between ideas.**

---

# 🤖 Role of AI

AI is intentionally restrained.

Constellate should not generate the user's thoughts.

### AI should not:

* generate ideas
* provide prompts by default
* interrupt wandering
* tell users what to think
* automatically create meaning
* automatically define relationships

### AI may eventually:

* surface forgotten Stars
* identify possible relationships
* highlight recurring themes
* suggest possible Constellations
* help users explore their existing Sky

The principle is:

> **AI suggests. The human decides.**

AI should help people see their thinking more clearly, not replace the thinking.

* creativity scores

The purpose of the POC is to test the **human behavior**, not maximize engagement.

---

# 🧪 POC Hypothesis

The core hypothesis is:

> **When people are given space to wander without being given stimuli, they can generate thoughts that are worth preserving; when those thoughts are externalized and spatially connected, new patterns become visible.**

The POC should test whether people naturally:

1. choose to wander,
2. complete wandering sessions,
3. capture thoughts afterward,
4. return to their Stars,
5. move and rearrange their thoughts,
6. connect seemingly separate thoughts,
7. create Constellations.

The most important signal is not the number of Stars.

It is whether users spontaneously say:

> **"Wait. These two things are connected."**

---

# 🔬 Research Questions

The POC should explore:

### Wandering

* Does giving people a choice of wandering activities make it easier to enter an unstructured thinking state?
* Does the app feel different from a productivity task?

### Capture

* Do users naturally want to preserve thoughts after wandering?
* Is capturing a Star fast enough that it doesn't disrupt the experience?

### Spatial thinking

* Does physically moving Stars change how users perceive their thoughts?
* Do users naturally create relationships without being instructed to?

### Constellations

* Do users see meaningful patterns across thoughts collected over time?
* Does a Constellation reveal something they hadn't previously noticed?

### Long-term value

* Does seeing an evolving Sky make users want to return?
* Does the Sky become a meaningful representation of their thinking?

# 🧱 Suggested Architecture

The POC can be built around a simple data model.

```text
User
 │
 ├── Wander Activities
 │
 ├── Wander Sessions
 │
 └── Sky
      │
      ├── Stars
      │    ├── content
      │    ├── name
      │    ├── timestamp
      │    └── originating session
      │
      ├── Connections
      │    ├── source Star
      │    ├── target Star
      │    └── direction
      │
      └── Constellations
           ├── name
           ├── Stars
           └── position
```

The data model should support:

```text
One Star → Multiple Constellations
One Constellation → Multiple Stars
One Star → Multiple Connections
```

This is important because human thinking is rarely hierarchical.

# 🌟 Brand Philosophy

The name **Constellate** comes from the act of arranging stars into a constellation.

A Star by itself is simply a point of light.

A constellation exists because **someone perceives a relationship between points of light.**

That is the central metaphor of the product.

> **Our thoughts are scattered like stars.
> Meaning emerges when we begin to see how they connect.**

> **Constellate is a personal thinking space where you wander freely, capture what emerges, and connect the thoughts that become meaningful together.**
